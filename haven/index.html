Event rules

Haven Hong Kong is a free, in-person game jam for teenagers. These are the rules for the weekend and for prizes, including Aseprite licenses.

  1. 1. Who can join

    You can join if you are 13 to 18. No experience is required. The jam is in person in Hong Kong on November 14–15, 2026, and it is free.

  2. 2. What you make

    You make a video game during the weekend. Any engine or tool is welcome, including Aseprite. You can work on your own or with friends.

  3. 3. How prizes are given

    Prizes and merch go to projects that ship a game other people can open and play on itch.io. A build that does not run does not qualify.

  4. 4. The game has to be yours

    Build it at the jam. Credit the asset packs, tutorials, and tools you use. Do not submit someone else’s game as your own.

  5. 5. What is not allowed

    No NSFW, hateful, or overly graphic content. The Hack Club code of conduct applies the whole weekend. Organizers can ask someone to leave if they break it.

  6. 6. Aseprite licenses

    An Aseprite license given as a prize is for one person. Do not resell it or share the installer. Art you make with Aseprite is yours. The Aseprite app stays under Aseprite’s license and cannot be redistributed.

  7. 7. Questions

    Parents, teens, and sponsors can write to hong-kong@haven.hackclub.com. The hour-by-hour schedule is still being set. Signing up is how you hear it first.

Hack Club code of conduct · hong-kong@haven.hackclub.com

FAQ

  • Am I eligible?

    If you’re age 13-18, you’re eligible! No prior experience required.

  • But I’ve never coded before!

    Perfect! Game jams are designed for beginners. You’ll have workshops, mentors, and teammates to help you every step of the way.

  • I still have questions!

    Join #haven-hong-kong on Slack or reach out to us at hong-kong@haven.hackclub.com!

  • Is this free?

    Yes! Hack Club is a nonprofit helping teens build technical projects at no cost.

  • What if my parents are concerned?

    We’re here to help! You can see our parent guide here, or they can reach out to us at hong-kong@haven.hackclub.com for questions.

Hack Club is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit and network of 100k+ technical high schoolers. We believe you learn best by building, so we’re creating community and providing grants so you can make awesome projects. In the past few years, we’ve sent 30 teen hackers hiking the Pacific Crest Trail, hosted a hackathon for the worst ideas, and ran the largest teen hardware hackathon at GitHub HQ.

Read about Hack Club in The Wall Street Journal, CBS News, and NASA.gov, or watch us on stage with AMD CEO Lisa Su at CES.
